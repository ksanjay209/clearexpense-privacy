ClearExpense privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026. Publisher: True Jyotish. Contact: support@truejyotish.org.

ClearExpense is an expense tracker that builds your spending record from the bank SMS on your phone. Your financial data stays on your phone. ClearExpense has no servers, no accounts and no advertising, and it does not send your data anywhere.

What ClearExpense reads

  • SMS messages. With your permission, ClearExpense reads the SMS inbox on your phone and checks each new SMS as it arrives, including when the app is closed. It uses messages from banks, cards, wallets and billers to record transactions, account balances, bills due and subscriptions. Messages from personal phone numbers are skipped.
  • What you type. Expenses, budgets, accounts and notes you add by hand.
  • Files you choose. A backup file, if you restore one.

Where your data is kept

Everything ClearExpense records is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. ClearExpense does not upload, share or sell SMS content, transactions or any other data, and it contains no analytics or advertising code.

Two things can copy the data off your phone, and both are under your control:

  • Backups you save. "Save backup" writes a file to the place you pick, such as Google Drive or Downloads. That copy is governed by the service you saved it to.
  • Android device backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include ClearExpense's data in the backup kept in your own Google account.

Permissions

  • SMS (read and receive): automatic expense tracking, as described above. Optional. Without it you can still add expenses by hand or paste messages.
  • Notifications: bill reminders, and an alert when a new bank SMS arrives.
  • Run at startup: to restore your bill reminders after the phone restarts.

Subscriptions

ClearExpense starts with a 5-day free trial that needs no payment details. After that, automatic tracking and bill reminders need a ClearExpense Premium subscription; adding expenses by hand and everything already recorded stay free. Subscriptions are bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment and ClearExpense never sees your card or bank details. The app keeps only the date the trial began and whether a subscription is active, on your phone. You can cancel at any time in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions.

Your choices

  • Turn SMS reading off in ClearExpense's Settings, or remove the permission in Android settings.
  • Erase everything with Settings, "Erase everything", or by uninstalling the app.
  • Hide amounts, set a PIN, and block screenshots in Settings.

Children

ClearExpense is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown here with a new effective date.
